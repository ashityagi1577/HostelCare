About The Platform

Why HostelCare? Purpose & Vision

HostelCare transforms traditional university accommodation maintenance into an efficient, transparent, and data-driven residential support system.

❌ The Traditional Hostel Challenge
  • ⚠️ Paper Complaint Registers: Requests handwritten in office notebooks get misplaced, smudged, or overlooked.
  • ⚠️ No Progress Visibility: Students have no way of knowing whether an electrician or plumber has been dispatched.
  • ⚠️ Misdiagnosed Faults: Verbal descriptions cause technicians to arrive without the proper spare parts or tools.
  • ⚠️ Lack of Accountability: No formal completion timestamps or quality checks prior to marking complaints resolved.
✔ The HostelCare Modern Solution
  • ✅ Digital Ticket Lifecycle: Unique tracking tickets (e.g. HC-2026-0001) generated immediately with permanent records.
  • ✅ Live 5-Stage Stepper: Real-time status tracker from Submitted ➔ Assigned ➔ In Progress ➔ Resolved ➔ Closed.
  • ✅ Photographic Verification: Students attach high-resolution photos directly so technicians inspect the exact issue.
  • ✅ Resident Quality Sign-Off: Repairs are only finalized after students test the fix and submit a 1-to-5 star quality rating.
End-to-End Operational Workflow

How HostelCare Resolves Maintenance

From the moment a resident notices a fault to the final verification sign-off, every action is tracked with timestamped precision.

1

Student Lodges Request

Resident logs in to their dashboard, chooses the exact discipline (Electrical, Plumbing, Carpentry, Cleaning, or Wi-Fi), provides room details, and uploads a photo of the damaged fixture.

📱 Student Dashboard
2

Warden Triage & Assign

The hostel warden reviews the submission on the staff triage desk, sets priority level (Low, Medium, High, or Emergency), estimates turnaround time, and dispatches an available specialist technician.

🛡️ Warden Triage Desk
3

Technician Field Repair

The technician accesses their dedicated panel in English or Hindi, marks the job "In Progress", executes on-site repairs, records work remarks/parts replaced, and updates status to "Resolved".

🔧 Technician Panel
4

Verification & Feedback

The student inspects the repair. Once fully verified, the resident signs off with a 1-to-5 star review and qualitative feedback, permanently closing the complaint.

⭐ Quality Assurance
